# Scientific Literature & Algorithm Citations

1. **Plane-Orthogonal-to-Skin (POS)**:
   - *Citation*: Wang, W., den Brinker, A. C., Stuijk, S., & de Haan, G. (2017). Algorithmic principles of remote PPG. *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*, 64(7), 1479-1491.
   - *AuraPulse Implementation*: core/rppg/pos.py, src/signal/pos.py

2. **Chrominance-Based Method (CHROM)**:
   - *Citation*: De Haan, G., & Jeanne, V. (2013). Robust pulse rate from chrominance-based rPPG. *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*, 60(10), 2878-2886.
   - *AuraPulse Implementation*: core/rppg/chrom.py, src/signal/chrom.py

3. **Green Channel Photoplethysmography**:
   - *Citation*: Verkruysse, W., Svaasand, L. O., & Nelson, J. S. (2008). Remote plethysmographic imaging using ambient light. *Optics Express*, 16(26), 21434-21445.
   - *AuraPulse Implementation*: core/rppg/green.py, src/signal/green.py

4. **Pulse Blood Volume (PBV)**:
   - *Citation*: De Haan, G., & van Leest, A. (2014). Improved motion robustness of remote-PPG by using the blood volume pulse signature. *Physiological Measurement*, 35(9), 1913.
   - *AuraPulse Implementation*: core/rppg/pbv.py, src/signal/pbv.py

5. **Baevsky Stress Index & Heart Rate Variability**:
   - *Citation*: Baevsky, R. M., & Chernikova, A. G. (2017). Heart rate variability analysis: physiological foundations and main methods. *Cardiometry*, (10), 66-76.
   - *AuraPulse Implementation*: core/vitals/stress.py, src/metrics/stress.py
