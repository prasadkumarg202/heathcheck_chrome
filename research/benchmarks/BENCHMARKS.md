# Benchmark Protocol & Synthetic Stress Tests

The AuraPulse benchmark suite evaluates:
1. **Fitzpatrick Skin Tone Scale (Types I–VI)**: Evaluating optical absorption modulation across melanin variance.
2. **Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) Noise Stress**: Adding Gaussian noise down to 0 dB and verifying fail-safe rejection when SQI falls below 35.
3. **Motion Amplitude Invariance**: Measuring angular head yaw/pitch tolerance.
