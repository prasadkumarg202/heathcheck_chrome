Clinical Reference Guidelines

Heart Rate (Pulse Rate)

Normal: 60 - 100 BPM (Resting). Tachycardia (>100) or Bradycardia (<60) may indicate physiological or autonomic stress.

Respiration Rate

Normal: 12 - 20 breaths/min. Tachypnea (>20) or Bradypnea (<12) reflects respiratory demand or autonomic shift.

Pulse Rhythm & Arrhythmia (Poincaré)

Normal: Regular Sinus Rhythm with Coefficient of Variation (CV) < 12%. Elevated CV (≥12%) with high SD1/SD2 ratio (>0.85) indicates irregular pulse rhythm.

Cardiorespiratory Fitness (VO2 max)

Non-exercise Jackson-Pollock regression combining age, sex, BMI, and RMSSD variability to classify aerobic fitness tiers.

Baevsky Stress Index (SI)

Standardized 50ms histogram mode. Eustress: 50-150 SI; Moderate Load: 151-300 SI; High Sympathetic Load: >300 SI.

Vascular Heart Age & 10-Yr CVD Risk

Anchored strictly to chronological baseline. Framingham General CVD Cox Proportional Hazard Model.
