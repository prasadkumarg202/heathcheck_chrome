Mandatory Safe-Harbor Guidelines for Commercial Wellness Mode

1. Universal Disclaimer: Every screen, printable summary, and exported report includes the clear safe-harbor notice: "AuraPulse is intended for informational, fitness, and general wellness purposes only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition or disease."
